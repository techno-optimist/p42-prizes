# Bounty

Submit a C4-free subgraph of the fixed hypercube `Q7`. The exact verifier
scores the number of submitted edges and exhaustively checks all 672 possible
four-cycles. The locked executable frontier is 304 edges, and settlement units
are raw edges: a qualifying improvement must have at least 305 edges.

The 304-edge seed proves only `ex(Q7,C4) >= 304`. Equality at 304 is
conjectural; this package supplies no upper-bound proof. This finite-record
board does not solve Erdős problem #86's asymptotic question and does not claim
Erdős's associated $100 prize.

Before any funding decision, the ordinary immutable-image, open-witness,
provenance, legal, and multi-host admission gates still apply.
