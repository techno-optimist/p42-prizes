# Provenance Boundary

The bundled `tests/seed-pg25.json` has SHA-256
`4c0e38ba9e174fe95c78de0acc2fa216ade92a52ba41b88ee010fbf7a61092ce`.
The local test suite exhausts every five-vertex subset and verifies that this
18-edge projective-plane construction has covering number six.

The pinned mathematical sources are Barát, arXiv `2011.04444v1` (upper
witness/projective subcase), and Sivashankar, arXiv `2606.24878v1` (general
lower bound). Their retrieved PDF hashes and DOI identifiers are recorded in
`docs/provenance/production-board-evidence-v1.json`.

This package certifies submitted finite hypergraphs only. It does not claim
that general `q(6)` equals 18, and source review does not substitute for the
independent math, legal, immutable-image, or N-host reviews required before
funding.
