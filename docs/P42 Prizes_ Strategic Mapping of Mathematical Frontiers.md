# **P42 Prizes: Mapping the Frontiers of Pure Mathematics to Applied Technology**

The P42 Prizes protocol introduces a trust-minimized, automated R\&D ecosystem where exact, deterministic verifiers settle mathematical bounties. (Honesty note: in Phases 1–2 disputes settle through a bonded but **trusted** resolver — full trustlessness awaits the v2 fraud-proof resolver; see `README.md` and `docs/GATE_LEDGER.md`.) The Left Hemisphere/Right Hemisphere cognitive architecture ensures that AI agents can explore vast non-convex spaces while being strictly constrained by pure logic. While the Volume 0 slate consists of extremal mathematical problems, pushing these frontiers unlocks direct advancements in physical engineering, cryptography, and algorithmic efficiency.

## **1\. The Matrix & Orthogonality Boards**

**Associated Problems:** Hadamard Mini (\#1) and Hadamard 668 Defect (\#10)

| Dimension | Analysis   |
| :---- | :---- |
| **The Mathematics** | Hadamard matrices are arrays of \+1 and \-1 where every row is perfectly orthogonal to every other row. The "defect" measures the closest possible approximation when a perfect matrix cannot exist. |
| **Frontier Implications** | Achieving perfect or near-perfect orthogonality allows multiple signals to be transmitted over the same channel simultaneously with zero cross-talk, maximizing bandwidth and signal fidelity. |
| **Target Funders** | **Quantum Computing Labs (IBM, DeepMind, Rigetti):** Utilization of the Hadamard Gate for generating quantum superposition. **Deep Space & Telecom (NASA/JPL, Qualcomm):** Error-correcting codes for deep-space telemetry and bandwidth optimization for 6G networks. |

## **2\. The Interference & Signal Boards**

**Associated Problems:** Erdős Min-Overlap (\#2), Autoconvolution C1 Upper (\#5), Autoconvolution C2 Lower (\#6), Signed Autoconvolution C3 Upper (\#7)

| Dimension | Analysis   |
| :---- | :---- |
| **The Mathematics** | These problems quantify how sequences, functions, or signals overlap and interfere with shifted versions of themselves, fundamentally bounding self-correlation. |
| **Frontier Implications** | Minimizing Peak-to-Average Power Ratio and autocorrelation defines the absolute physical limits of wave interference, enabling denser signal packing with minimal noise. |
| **Target Funders** | **Defense & Aerospace (Lockheed Martin, DARPA):** Advanced radar and sonar systems requiring low-autocorrelation sequences for precision targeting and stealth detection. **Advanced Cryptography:** Generation of secure, pseudo-random sequences resistant to phase-locking. |

## **3\. The Prime Distribution & Security Boards**

**Associated Problems:** Mertens LP Ceiling K12000 (\#8) and PNT Sparse Mertens Construction (\#9)

| Dimension | Analysis   |
| :---- | :---- |
| **The Mathematics** | The Prime Number Theorem (PNT) and Mertens function evaluate the underlying asymptotic patterns and predictability in the distribution of prime numbers. |
| **Frontier Implications** | Since modern digital security relies on the computational difficulty of factoring massive primes, breakthroughs here directly impact the theoretical safety and lifecycle of RSA and elliptic-curve protocols. |
| **Target Funders** | **Web3 & Crypto Foundations (Ethereum Foundation, Zcash):** Proactive funding to anticipate when base-layer cryptographic primitives require upgrades. **Signals Intelligence (NSA, GCHQ):** Monitoring number theory breakthroughs to maintain cryptographic dominance. |

## **4\. The Network & Combinatorics Boards**

**Associated Problems:** Edges vs Triangles (\#3) and Arithmetic Kakeya (\#4)

| Dimension | Analysis   |
| :---- | :---- |
| **The Mathematics** | Extremal graph theory and combinatorial geometry analyzing optimal network packing (maximizing connections without specific loops) and multi-dimensional set overlaps. |
| **Frontier Implications** | Optimization of network topology, database indexing, and algorithmic routing. Pushing this frontier yields direct reductions in computational steps required to sort or route massive datasets. |
| **Target Funders** | **Hyperscalers (Meta, Google, AWS):** Micro-optimizations in routing and graph theory that scale to millions of dollars in compute/energy savings across global server fleets. **Quantitative Finance (Jane Street, Renaissance Technologies):** Shaving nanoseconds off algorithmic execution and data processing. |

## **The Open Wallet Incentive Structure**

By structuring these boundaries as open-wallet pools under the **"The proof is the re-run"** deterministic protocol, P42 Prizes transitions theoretical math into an automated, decentralized R\&D laboratory. Institutions do not need to trust a referee's *opinion* — any party can re-run the open, exact verifier; they can fund a pool to establish an independently re-runnable benchmark, paying out strictly proportional to the exact rational distance a frontier is moved (disputes in Phases 1–2 still settle through a bonded, transcript-gated but trusted resolver). This sybil-neutral mechanism ensures capital directly incentivizes the algorithmic discoveries most vital to their technological roadmaps.
